# Compare editions: Free, Standard, Premium, Enterprise

What each edition includes:

- Free: up to 10 users, 5 automation rules, 2 integrations. No single sign-on, no guests, no Relay Assist.
- Standard: 15 automation rules, 3 integrations, single sign-on, guests, and 10 Relay Assist credits per seat per month if you add Relay Assist.
- Premium: 60 automation rules, 10 integrations, single sign-on, guests, and 40 Assist credits per seat per month.
- Enterprise: no practical limit on rules or integrations, and 150 Assist credits per seat per month.

This article describes features only. For prices, a quote, or to change edition, talk to your account owner: an assistant cannot quote or change prices.
