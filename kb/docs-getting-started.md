# Get started with Relay Docs

Relay Docs is a knowledge base next to your boards: meeting notes, wiki pages, how-to guides and decisions. Pages live in spaces, and every page can link to the cards it is about.

If your team keeps meeting notes or wiki pages in boards today, Docs gives you proper pages, a table of contents, search across everything, and page history. To start, an admin adds Relay Docs under Admin settings, Products. Then create a space, pick the Meeting notes or Wiki template, and move your first notes over.
