# Invite your team

Admins invite people from Admin settings, Users, Invite. Type one email address per line or paste a list. Each invited person receives an email with a link that is valid for seven days.

You can invite as many people as you have licensed seats. A seat is used as soon as the invitation is sent, and freed again if you cancel the invitation or deactivate the user.

Tip: teams that invite at least five people in their first week are far more likely to keep using Relay, because boards are only useful when the work of several people is on them.
