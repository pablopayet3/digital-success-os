# Connect an integration

Integrations connect Relay to the other tools your team uses. Available integrations: chat, calendar, code repository, cloud drive, CRM and video meetings.

To connect one (admins only), open Admin settings, Integrations, choose the tool and click Connect. You are sent to that tool to approve access, then returned to Relay. The integration is live straight away for the whole workspace.

How many integrations you can connect depends on your edition: 2 on Free, 3 on Standard, 10 on Premium, and no practical limit on Enterprise. Connecting at least one integration is one of the four setup steps we recommend in the first week.
