# Boards are slow to load

Very large boards (more than about 2,000 open cards) take longer to load. Three things help: archive cards in the Done list that are older than a month (an automation rule can do this for you); split the board by team or by quarter; and switch off card cover images under Board settings, Appearance.

If a board with fewer than 500 cards takes more than a few seconds, that is not expected. Contact support with the name of the board so an engineer can look at it.
