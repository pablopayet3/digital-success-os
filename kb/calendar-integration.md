# Show due dates in your calendar

With the calendar integration, every card that has a due date and an assignee appears in that person's calendar. Connect it under Admin settings, Integrations, Calendar.

If due dates do not show up after connecting, check three things: the card has both a due date and an assignee; the assignee has accepted the calendar permission request they received by email; and the board is not archived. New and changed dates can take up to fifteen minutes to appear.
