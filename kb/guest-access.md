# Give an external person access to one board (guests)

You can invite a contractor or client as a guest. Open the board, click Share, type their email address and choose Guest. A guest sees only the boards they were invited to, and cannot see the list of users or any other board.

Guests do not use a licensed seat on Standard, Premium and Enterprise. On the Free edition, guests are not available.

To remove a guest, open Share on the board and remove them, or deactivate them under Admin settings, Users, Guests.
