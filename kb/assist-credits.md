# How Relay Assist credits work

Relay Assist is the AI assistant inside Relay. Each use spends credits: a summary of a card or document uses 1 to 4 credits depending on length, a draft uses 3 to 8, and a search across the workspace uses 1 to 3.

Your workspace receives a monthly allowance of credits per licensed seat, set by your edition (10 on Standard, 40 on Premium, 150 on Enterprise). Credits are shared across the workspace and reset on the first day of each month. Unused credits do not carry over.

Admins can see usage under Admin settings, Relay Assist. If you run out before the end of the month, Assist stops until the reset. To get more credits, talk to your account owner.
