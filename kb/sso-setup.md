# Set up single sign-on (SSO)

SSO lets your team sign in to Relay with your company identity provider. It is available on the Standard, Premium and Enterprise editions. It is not available on Free.

Steps (admins only):
1. Open Admin settings, then Security, then Single sign-on.
2. Choose the protocol your identity provider uses: SAML or OIDC.
3. For SAML, upload the identity provider metadata file (XML). For OIDC, paste the client ID, client secret and issuer URL.
4. Click Test connection. Relay signs you in through the provider in a new window.
5. When the test succeeds, click Enable for everyone.

Keep one admin account with password sign-in until you have confirmed SSO works for the whole team. SSO setup usually takes ten to fifteen minutes.
