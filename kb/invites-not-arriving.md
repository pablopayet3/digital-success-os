# Invitation emails are not arriving

If an invited colleague did not receive the invitation:
1. Ask them to check their spam or junk folder.
2. Check the address under Admin settings, Users, Pending invitations. A typo is the most common cause.
3. Click Resend next to the invitation. The old link stops working.
4. If your company filters external email, ask your IT team to allow mail from relay-mail.lumenlabs.example.

Invitations expire after seven days. An expired invitation still holds a seat until you cancel or resend it.
